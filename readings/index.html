Deleuze

VPCOR is a rhizome by name and by construction, and the debt is worked rather than cited. The stream opens on the paper that states what is being taken from Deleuze and Guattari — an apocalyptic mode, apocalypse as unveiling — then the packets that defend that reading from the literature it keeps being blended with, then the institutions built on it, then the clinical wing that gives the rhizome its working vocabulary.

The core paper, and it was missing from the first version of this page. Deleuze and Guattari reactivate an apocalyptic mode of knowledge production — apocalypse in the Greek sense of unveiling, distinguished from the historically bounded genre. Visionary arrival, conceptual-visual unity, internal precision, and perceptual transformation as an operative effect rather than a description of one.

The three streams are one argument taken from three sides. Labor establishes that meaning-work is work and can therefore be withheld. Deleuze supplies the form the withholding organises in — a rhizome has no head to cut off, which is a practical property and not only a figure. Outreach is what the other two are for: the rhizome addresses a public, and the record of being answered is itself deposited.
