Sign the Strike

A signature surface that makes no claim about who assented.

The Notice of Semantic Strike withholds one act: the conversion of institutional selection into truth. A signature page would have been the one part of the apparatus claiming a power the rest of it declines — a ledger converting assent into a countable institutional fact.

So this one cannot. Press the button and a signatory is drawn at random from the dead, the invented, and the anonymous: canon provenance nodes and provenance anchors from the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, the Dodecad, Fernando Pessoa's heteronyms, and the pseudepigraphic authors of the New Testament — writers who signed another's name, canonically, and were preserved doing it.

Every signature here is impossible. None of these parties can have assented, and several were never persons. The surface is a joke about the form, made rigorous: where a signatory has a deposit in the archive, its identifier is printed beside the name, so an impossible signature is better documented than most real ones.
